Getting your domain

Your domain is your website's address, like yourbusiness.com. You buy it yourself so it's always in your name, and I connect it to your website for free. It takes about 10 minutes, and this page walks you through every step.

The short version
  • Cost: usually $11–15 a year for a .com, paid to the domain company (not to me).
  • You own it: it's in your name, on your account. I never hold your domain.
  • I connect it for free: you never need to give me your password or card.
  • Live in: usually under an hour after we connect it, sometimes up to 48 hours.

What you'll need

    • About 10 minutes
    • A debit or credit card (about $11–15)
    • An email address you check, plus your phone for a verification code
    • 2 or 3 name ideas, in case your first choice is taken

Step by step

  1. 1
    You

    Pick your name

    Go with your business name if you can. A few tips:

    • Choose .com if it's available. It's what people trust and type by habit.
    • Keep it short and easy to spell out loud. Skip dashes and numbers.
    • If your name is taken, try adding your town or what you do, like smithplumbingmn.com or getsmithplumbing.com.

    Not sure? Text me a few ideas and I'll tell you which one I'd pick.

  2. 2
    You

    Buy it

    I recommend Porkbun.com. It's simple, has honest pricing, and includes privacy protection at no extra cost. Cloudflare and Namecheap are good too. If you'd rather use GoDaddy, that works, but expect a lot of add-on offers.

    • Search your name and add the .com to your cart.
    • Choose 1 year. Check the renewal price so there are no surprises next year.
    • Make your account with your own email, then pay with your own card.
  3. 3
    You

    Skip the extras

    Domain sites try to sell you things you don't need. You only need the domain itself.

    Buy

    • Your domain (.com)
    • Auto-renew turned on
    • Privacy protection (if it's free)

    Skip

    • Web hosting (I host it free)
    • Website builders
    • SSL or security add-ons (included free)
    • Extra endings like .net or .org
    • Paid email hosting (see below)
  4. 4
    You

    Turn on auto-renew and keep your login safe

    Turn on auto-renew and keep your card up to date. If a domain expires, someone else can buy it. Save your login somewhere safe, and turn on two-step verification if they offer it.

  5. 5
    You

    Text or email me

    Just send me the domain you bought, like "I got smithplumbing.com on Porkbun." That's it. Never send me your password or card info.

  6. 6
    Me

    We connect it together

    I'll send you a few short settings to copy and paste into your domain account, with exactly where to tap. Or we can hop on a quick call or screen share and I'll walk you through it live. It takes about 5 minutes.

  7. 7
    Me

    I finish the setup and test it

    • I point your website to your new address.
    • I turn on the secure padlock (https) so browsers never say "Not Secure."
    • I make sure both yourbusiness.com and www.yourbusiness.com work.
    • I test it on phones and computers, then text you when it's live.

    It's usually live in under an hour. Sometimes it takes up to 48 hours to work everywhere, and that's normal.

Common questions

I already own a domain. What do I do?

Great, you don't need a new one and you don't need to move it. Just tell me where you bought it (GoDaddy, Squarespace, Wix, Google, etc.). We'll update a few settings together and it'll point to your new site. Don't remember where it is? Tell me the domain and I'll help you find out.

Will my old website or email stop working?

When we connect your domain, it will show your new website instead of the old one. If you already have email at your domain (like you@yourbusiness.com), I'll make sure your email settings stay in place so your email keeps working.

Can I get an email like me@mybusiness.com?

Yes. The easiest option is free email forwarding: anything sent to you@yourbusiness.com shows up in the email you already use, like Gmail. Porkbun and Cloudflare include this at no extra cost, and I'll set it up with you. If you want a full separate inbox, Google Workspace is a paid option, and I'll help you decide if it's worth it.

Why don't you just buy the domain for me?

So it's always yours. When the domain is in your name, on your account, you're never stuck with me or anyone else. That's how it should be.

Do you need my password?

No, never. You stay logged in on your own account, and I just tell you what to copy and paste. I'll never ask for your password or card details.

What if I get stuck?

Text me a screenshot of what you're seeing and I'll tell you exactly what to tap. That's what I'm here for.
