How edits work

Everything about changing your website after it launches, in one place: what counts as a small edit, what it costs with or without a care plan, and what happens when you send a request.

The short version
  • Before launch: 2 full rounds of changes are included in your website price.
  • After launch: small edits are included in every care plan. Without a plan, they're $35 each.
  • Extra edits beyond what your plan includes are also $35 each.
  • Bigger changes like new sections, new pages or redesigns are always quoted up front.

What counts as a small edit

A small edit is a quick update to what's already on your site that takes about 15 minutes or less.

Small edits

  • Fixing a typo
  • Changing your business hours
  • Updating a price
  • Swapping an old photo for a new one
  • Small text changes to an existing section

Quoted separately

  • Adding a new section to a page
  • Building a new page ($50 each)
  • Redesigning the look or layout of a page
  • New features like booking or online stores

How a request works

  1. Send it over. Text or email me what you'd like changed. Screenshots help.
  2. I confirm what it is. I'll tell you if it's a small edit, or send you a price if it's a bigger change.
  3. Payment, if needed. If it isn't covered by your plan, I send a secure payment link. Work starts once it's paid.
  4. I make the change and let you know when it's live.

The rules, in plain words

  • No plan: the $35 fee is paid before any work begins.
  • Who goes first: care plan members' edits always come first. Requests without a plan are done when there's an opening in my schedule.
  • Use it or lose it: plan edits reset every month and don't save up.
  • Before launch: your first 2 rounds of changes are included in your website price ($400 refresh or $700 full build). After that, extra rounds are quoted and paid up front, or you can walk away and owe nothing.
