Getting started

How does it work?

We start with a quick call about your business. I build your website and show it to you, usually in about 1 week. If you love it, you pay and I launch it. If it's not for you, you walk away and owe nothing.

Do I pay anything up front?

No. You don't pay a cent until you've seen your finished site and love it.

How long does it take?

Most sites are ready to show you in about 1 week, as long as I get your text and photos within a few days of our first call.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo (if you have one), photos, your business info, and anything you'd like the site to say. Don't have much written? No problem. I'll help write it for free.

Is it just you?

Yes. I'm a one-person studio, so you work with me directly from start to finish. I only take a few projects at a time so every client gets my full attention.

Pricing

How much does a website cost?

A Full Website is $700 and a Website Refresh is $400. My first 5 clients get $100 off ($600 or $300) in exchange for an honest review.

What counts as a page?

A page is one section of your site with its own link, like Home, About, Services, Gallery or Contact. Both packages include up to 5 pages, which is plenty for most local businesses.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The only other cost is your domain name (about $15 a year), which you buy yourself so it's always yours. I don't use paid plugins, and if a feature ever needs a paid tool, I'll tell you the cost first.

What if I need more?

More pages, an online store, booking, online payments or other features can be added. I'll give you a price up front before doing anything extra.

How do I pay?

When you approve your finished site, I send you a secure payment link. You pay by card, and then I launch your site.

Hosting, domain and ownership

Is hosting included?

Yes, hosting is free. Every site also comes with secure https (the padlock), so browsers never show a "Not Secure" warning.

Do I need to buy a domain?

Yes. A domain is your website's name, like yourbusiness.com. You buy it yourself (about $15 a year) so it's always in your name and under your control. I'll walk you through it step by step and connect it to your site for free.

Who owns my website?

You do, once it's paid for. Your site is built with standard code that any web designer can edit, and if you ever want to move on, I'll hand over all your files.

What if you stop doing websites?

You'll never be stuck. If I ever stop offering care plans, you get at least 60 days' notice and everything you need to keep your site running.

Changes and care

What are "2 rounds of changes"?

After you see your site, you can send me a list of changes. I make them, show you again, and you get one more round after that. A round is one list sent together.

What do monthly care plans include?

Basic ($39) covers updates, backups, downtime monitoring and 1 small edit a month. Growth ($99) adds up to 5 edits, monthly Google and speed checks, and a report. Pro ($199) adds new pages or posts, seasonal promos and priority support. Cancel anytime.

Do I need a care plan?

No. It's optional. Without one, your site still stays online for free. A care plan just means I handle updates and changes so you never have to.

Do unused edits roll over?

No. Edits reset each month. An edit is a small change like new text, hours, prices or a photo swap.

Google, security and accessibility

Will my site show up on Google?

I set up the basics so Google can find and understand your site: page titles, descriptions and fast mobile loading. I can also help with your free Google Business Profile. But no one can honestly guarantee a #1 ranking. That takes time.

Is my website accessible?

Every site is built with accessibility basics: readable colors, screen-reader labels and keyboard-friendly navigation.

What about privacy?

Every site includes a simple privacy policy page. Sites don't use tracking cookies unless you ask for them.

What if my site gets hacked or goes down?

Simple, modern sites like mine are very hard to hack, and I keep care-plan sites updated. If anything ever breaks, I'll work with you to fix it fast. See my project terms for details.

Ready to see your website?

No money up front. You only pay if you love it.
