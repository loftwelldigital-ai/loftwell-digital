My story

School didn't fit me. Building things did.

Traditional classrooms didn't fit the way my mind works. I was homeschooled, so I learned early how to motivate myself, manage my own time and work through hard problems on my own.

Instead of waiting for someone to hand me a plan, I put that energy into web design and taught myself to build custom websites from scratch. That same drive is what Loftwell Digital is built on. Every business owner who calls me has built something special, and my job is to help the world see it.

I treat every project like a partnership, so I only take a few clients at a time and you get my full attention. And because I build your site first and you only pay if you love it, I'm fully invested in earning your trust and making a website we're both proud of.
